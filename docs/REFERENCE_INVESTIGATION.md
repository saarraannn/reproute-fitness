# Reference-site investigation

Research date: 22 November 2026. The following review uses publicly accessible official pages and their published content. The notes are design observations, not claims that RepRoute has the same features or services.

## 1. Nike Training Club
- **Reference:** https://www.nike.com/ntc-app
- **Observed:** The page introduces the training product, explains workout categories and coaching content, and presents programmes as progressive ways to train. Nike describes strength, HIIT, yoga and bodyweight workouts, plus programmes and coaching.
- **Useful pattern:** Lead with a clear promise, then organise training by activity and programme.
- **Potential difficulty:** The page primarily promotes an app download; visitors looking for a direct, locally bookable class may need to move to another experience.
- **RepRoute response:** Keep the catalogue and booking journey directly on the site, and show category, level, duration and price on each card.
- **Evidence:** Capture the official NTC landing page at the hero and programme sections.

## 2. ClassPass
- **Reference:** https://help.classpass.com/hc/en-us/articles/204312229-How-do-I-search-for-classes
- **Observed:** ClassPass documents search filters for activity, location, distance, credit rate, skill level and studio amenities. Its reservation guide explains the progression from searching to reviewing class details and reserving.
- **Useful pattern:** Filters help people narrow a broad set of fitness choices; booking should follow a review of the class details.
- **Potential difficulty:** Credit-based pricing and location/amenity filters add complexity for a visitor who only wants to compare a small set of programmes.
- **RepRoute response:** Use a simpler filter set for category, experience and price sorting, then provide a direct programme detail page.
- **Evidence:** Capture the ClassPass search help article and reservation guidance.

## 3. Peloton
- **Reference:** https://www.onepeloton.com/classes
- **Observed:** The class library groups content by activity and equipment context. Individual classes show duration and level; collections group related classes into longer sequences. The strength category exposes filters such as duration and class type.
- **Useful pattern:** Put level and duration near the class title and make filters visible close to the results.
- **Potential difficulty:** The library spans many modalities and devices, so first-time visitors may need to narrow the choice before comparing individual classes.
- **RepRoute response:** Keep the number of choices to ten and show schedule, duration, level, capacity and price on the detail page.
- **Evidence:** Capture the class library and strength category/filter view.
